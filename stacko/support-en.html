Stacko — Support

Developer: Martin Orlov · Updated July 27, 2026

Contact us

The fastest way to reach us is email: martinorlov013@gmail.com. We reply within 1–2 business days.

To help us solve your issue faster, please include:

  • Your device model and iOS version (Settings → General → About)
  • The version of Stacko (shown on the App Store page or in the App’s settings)
  • What you did, what you expected, and what happened instead
  • A screenshot or screen recording, if possible

Frequently asked questions

My progress disappeared

Progress is saved on your device. It can be lost if the App is deleted, if the device is reset, or if iCloud sync was disabled. If you restored a backup and the save did not come back, email us with your approximate last score and the date — we will help where we can.

How do I remove the ads?

If the App offers a “Remove ads” purchase, open the store screen inside the App and select it. Already paid on another device or after reinstalling? Use Restore Purchases on the same screen while signed in with the same Apple Account.

I paid but did not receive the item

Try Restore Purchases first. If that does not help, send us the App Store receipt (you get it by email from Apple, or via reportaproblem.apple.com) and we will sort it out.

The rewarded ad did not give my reward

Rewards are granted only when the ad is watched to the end and the network confirms it. If a video failed to load or you lost connection mid-way, close and reopen the App and try again. Persistent problems: email us with the approximate time it happened.

How do I change my advertising / tracking choice?

Open Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking on your device and toggle Stacko. If you are in the EEA or the UK, you can also reopen the consent screen from the App’s settings.

The App crashes or freezes

Update to the latest version from the App Store, restart your device, and make sure you have free storage space. If it still crashes, tell us exactly when it happens — that usually pinpoints it.

Requesting deletion of my data

See our Privacy Policy or write to martinorlov013@gmail.com.

Feature requests and bug reports

We read everything. Send ideas to martinorlov013@gmail.com — concrete suggestions have a good chance of showing up in a future update.
